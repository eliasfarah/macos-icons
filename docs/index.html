Why macOS Icons?

Quick Installation

Get the premium glassmorphism experience on your Linux desktop in seconds.

mkdir -p ~/.local/share/icons
ln -s $(pwd) ~/.local/share/icons/macos-icons
touch ~/.local/share/icons/macos-icons/.icon-theme.cache

Then select macos-icons in your appearance manager (GNOME Tweaks, KDE, etc.).
